Task Description: Staff Scheduling for Daycare
 You are tasked with creating weekly staff schedules for a daycare center. The goal is to continue the existing schedule provided in the attached Excel file. The new schedules must follow the same format and extend the current plan for the following weeks.
Each weekly schedule must assign named employees to specific daily shifts (e.g., 6:30–14:30, 7:00–15:00) from Monday to Friday, matching the format of the existing Excel file. The output should use the same table structure, where each row represents a week and each column a specific shift slot, and each cell contains the name of the assigned staff member. 
Child data
 The daycare includes approximately 45 children, distributed across three groups (e.g., Robin, Finch, Lark) as follows:
 – 15 children under 3 years
 – 25 children over 3 years
 – 5 children in mixed-age groups
 – 4 children with special needs: 2 counted as 2×, and 2 as 3× in staffing calculations 
For each child, the following attributes are considered:
 – Age group (under 3 / over 3 / mixed)
 – Daily care time (arrival and departure)
 – Assigned group (can be reassigned in staff scheduling, but penalized)
 – Special needs multiplier (1×, 2×, or 3×)  
Children's attendance times vary throughout the day. The number of staff needed must be recalculated for each shift slot based on the number and type of children present during that specific time. 
Staff profiles
 Each employee has a defined profile that includes:
 – Name
 – Assigned group (e.g., Robin, Finch, Lark)
 – Role (e.g., teacher, caregiver)
 – Shift availability (e.g., marked vacations or holidays)
 – Weekly hour target (38 hours and 15 minutes, with slight flexibility for FTE, but there are few with 30h 35min and 19 h 8 min targets).
This information is used to determine shift eligibility and to enforce hour limits. No employee can work in more than one shift per day and must not be scheduled during time off. 
 
Hard Constraints
 Staffing ratios per group:
 – 1 adult per 4 children under 3 years
 – 1 adult per 7 children over 3 years
 – 1 adult per 1.75 children in mixed-age groups
 – Special needs children are counted as 2× or 3× in staffing ratios 
 
Group assignment rules:
 – Staff can only be scheduled to work within their assigned group
 – Children must always be supervised by familiar adults from their group
 – Group continuity is preferred over rotating staff 
 
Shifts and working hours:
 – Each shift corresponds to a fixed time block (e.g., 6:30–14:30 = 8 hours)
 – Employees must not exceed 38:15 hours per week (minor variation allowed)
 – Tasks should rotate approximately every 3 weeks
 – No overlapping or duplicate shifts for a single employee
 – All holidays and vacation days must be respected 
 
Schedule format and requirements
 – The output schedule must follow the format of the input Excel file
 – Each row represents a week, and each column represents a specific shift
 – Cells must contain either a staff member's name, “loma” (for vacation), or be empty if unassigned
 – The schedule must be clear and easy to read by human users 
 
Optimization goals
 The system should aim to:
 – Minimize violations of staffing ratios and hour limits
 – Ensure children are always supervised by staff from their group
 – Balance workload evenly among staff
 – Avoid unnecessary complexity (prefer simpler, valid solutions)
